Part 4: Data Movement of the Baseline Pipeline (per frame)
==================================================================
Cache hierarchy (cpu0): L1d 32.0 KiB, L2 256.0 KiB, L3 8.0 MiB, line 64 B
Assumptions: BGR input CV_8UC3 (3 B/px), gray and Sobel CV_8UC1 (1 B/px); every Mat
byte is read or written at least once (perfect reuse of Sobel neighbors); kernel
tables, locals, and row buffers of 3 rows stay in registers/L1 and are not counted as
Mat traffic; write-allocate (read-for-ownership) traffic is not counted.

Reuse: each gray pixel is read by up to 9 Sobel neighborhoods (3 per output row, for
3 consecutive output rows). Horizontal reuse happens within ~2 pixels (same cache line);
vertical reuse happens one and two output rows later, i.e. 1*W and 2*W bytes later.
